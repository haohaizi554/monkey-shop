# WS3 Marketing Center

## Goal

Upgrade MonkeyShop from one-off order discounts to a real marketing center covering coupons, seckill, and group-buy workflows. This work stream follows WS1 catalog and WS2 inventory, so its migrations continue at V24-V26 even though the original roadmap named V23-V25 before the inventory stream consumed V22-V23.

## Business Scope

- Coupons support threshold, percent, category, and shop scopes.
- Coupon lifecycle is claim -> redeem -> return, with idempotent duplicate claim and return handling.
- Price quotes support platform and shop coupon stacking while keeping the payable amount above zero.
- Seckill uses independent activity stock, per-user purchase limits, Redis SETNX style idempotency, Turnstile-aware validation, and a distributed lock named by activity.
- Group-buy creates or joins teams, succeeds once the target size is reached, and cancels expired open teams through ShedLock.
- The honeypot path `/api/seckill/internal/active` remains a trap endpoint and is blocked by the shared rate-limit filter.

## Invariants

- Seckill sold quantity can never exceed activity stock.
- A user can claim a coupon once per coupon definition.
- Coupon return is idempotent and never recreates a consumed discount twice.
- Group-buy teams move only from OPEN to SUCCEEDED or CANCELLED.
- All marketing IDs are generated by the shared Snowflake `IdGenerator`.
- All write flows are transactional and audited through `AuditService`.
- Cross-service style calls are annotated with OpenTelemetry `@WithSpan`.

## Flyway

- `V24__marketing_coupon.sql` creates coupon definitions and user coupon ledgers.
- `V25__marketing_seckill.sql` creates seckill activities and order ledgers.
- `V26__marketing_group_buy.sql` creates group-buy activities, teams, and members.

## Acceptance

- 1000 concurrent seckill attempts against 10 units produce exactly 10 successes.
- Repeated coupon claim with the same user and coupon is idempotent.
- Platform and shop coupons can stack, while same-scope coupons are exclusive.
- Group-buy teams expire automatically and idempotently.
- Turnstile-enabled environments reject missing or invalid seckill tokens.
- `/api/seckill/internal/active` is treated as a honeypot by the shared rate limiter.
